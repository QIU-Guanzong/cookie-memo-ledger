What is included

Cookie Chain RPC health and confirmed-slot reads, strict Nightly detection, public-address display, and an opt-in signing path.

What is deliberately absent

No private-key input, automatic connection, background signing, account creation, bridge flow, deployment, token transfer, or data collection.
